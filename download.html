Being prepared

Download for Mac

Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
  • Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without a fuss.
  • No account, no sign-in. The app never contacts a server of ours except to check for new versions and, once you enter a key, to check your licence; the privacy page lists exactly what is sent.

Installing

  1. Unzip the download and drag MediaFlowswift into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. The 14-day trial of Studio Pro starts the first time it opens.
  3. If you use a NAS or a shared drive, macOS asks once whether the app may use your local network. Say yes; the manual explains why.

Updating
